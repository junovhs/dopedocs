Daily use

Find comfortable light

Lumen Desk adjusts with the brightness dial and the warm-light button.

Turn the dial slowly until the page is easy to read. Then start a focus session.

  1. Place the lamp

    Keep the base on a level surface.

  2. Choose the light

    Turn the dial and press the warmth button once.

WarningKeep the fictional lamp away from water. Follow your real product's safety instructions.

Care and help

Keep the lamp ready

Lumen Desk's example cleaning routine uses a soft, dry cloth after the lamp is unplugged and cool.

  1. Unplug the lamp.
  2. Wait until it is cool.
  3. Wipe with a soft, dry cloth.
The light does not turn on

Check the plug and try the power button. Stop using a damaged cable.
