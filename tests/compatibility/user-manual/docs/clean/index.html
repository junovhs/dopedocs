Keep the lamp ready

Lumen Desk's example cleaning routine uses a soft, dry cloth after the lamp is unplugged and cool.

  1. Unplug the lamp.
  2. Wait until it is cool.
  3. Wipe with a soft, dry cloth.
The light does not turn on

Check the plug and try the power button. Stop using a damaged cable.
